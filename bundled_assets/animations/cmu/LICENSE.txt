CMU motion-capture data — redistribution terms (not CC0)

These five FBX motion files contain human motion-capture data originally
created by the Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab. The database received
funding from NSF grant EIA-0196217. cgspeed supplied a BVH conversion; RancidMilk
converted those animations to FBX. This project obtained the bones-only files
from the gbionics/cmu-fbx mirror on Hugging Face, which is not the original
data author or the converter.

The CMU database permits using, copying, modifying and redistributing the data
without requesting permission, including use in commercial products. Direct
sale of the motion data itself is prohibited, including sale after conversion
to another format. RancidMilk also permits use, modification and redistribution
of its converted animations, including modified versions and other formats,
subject to the same restriction on selling the animations themselves.
Redistributors must make these conditions apparent to recipients and pass them
along. Keep this notice with these files when redistributing them.
